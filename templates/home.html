Featured story · Photojournalism

The Power Behind the Lens: Telling Stories with Care

Every photograph tells a story. The best journalism does more than make people look—it helps audiences understand what is happening, why it matters and whose voices deserve to be heard.

For student journalists, a camera is both a creative tool and a responsibility. Consider the context: who is in the frame, what the image communicates and whether publishing it could put someone at risk. Ask for consent where appropriate, especially when photographing children or people in vulnerable situations.

Strong photojournalism is honest. Avoid staging events or editing images in ways that change their meaning. Write accurate captions, check names and locations, and give people the dignity of being represented fairly.

Look beyond the lens. Tell the truth. Keep people at the heart of every story.
