Who we are

Journalism with community at its heart.

Ushirika means togetherness. Our journalism club is a place for aspiring reporters, storytellers, photographers and media makers to learn from one another and create work rooted in the lives of the people around them.

Through practical training, mentorship and collaborative reporting, we help members build strong foundations in ethical journalism and grow a portfolio they can be proud of.

What we do

Reporting workshops

02 / COMMUNITY

Community storytelling

03 / GROWTH

Mentorship & portfolio

Get feedback, connect with fellow media makers and build a portfolio that shows how your voice is growing.
